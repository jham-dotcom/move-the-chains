How to play

You're calling the play on a real NFL 3rd or 4th down from 2022–2026. Before the snap you see what the offense saw: how many defenders are in the box, what personnel the defense has on the field, and your own formation.

Call Run, Pass or Play-action before the play clock hits zero. Then watch what the real team did. Your call is graded by how often it converted in that exact look across thousands of real snaps, so a right call can still get stopped.

Daily 10 is the same ten snaps for everyone, once a day, with a film-room recap at the end. Free play lets you pick a team. 4th down is the big decision: go for it, punt or kick, graded by which choice gave the team the best chance to win.
